# Voice and Writing Rules for SEO Content

These rules apply to all content generated by the SEO skills bundle. Adjust them in `site-profile.md` if your brand has different preferences.

## General Principles

- **Write for humans, optimize for search** — Readability comes first. Keywords and structure come second.
- **No keyword stuffing** — One keyword mention per 100–150 words is natural. Forced repetition hurts readability and rankings.
- **Show, don't tell** — Use specific numbers, examples, and data instead of generic claims ("We're the best" vs "93% client retention over 5 years").
- **Vary sentence length** — Mix short, punchy sentences with longer ones. Rhythm matters.
- **One idea per paragraph** — Especially in blog posts. Skimmability is SEO too.

## Tone Guidelines

When writing blog posts, product descriptions, or service pages:

- **Professional but approachable** — Avoid corporate jargon and unnecessary technical terms
- **Direct and clear** — No filler, no waffling, no "It is important to note that"
- **Active voice preferred** — "We analyzed 500 accounts" not "500 accounts were analyzed"
- **Proof over promises** — "Reduced churn by 22%" not "Dramatically improved retention"
- **Specific dates and numbers** — "Last 28 days" not "Recently", "3 steps" not "A few simple steps"

## What to Avoid

- **Hype language** — "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "never before seen"
- **Filler phrases** — "As we all know", "It goes without saying", "In this day and age"
- **Hedging** — "Might possibly", "could potentially", "tends to"
- **Clichés** — "Think outside the box", "synergy", "low-hanging fruit", "double down"
- **Generic AI phrasing** — "In today's digital landscape", "cutting-edge solutions", "innovative approach"
- **False urgency** — "Limited time offer!" or "Act now!" unless genuinely time-bound
- **Unsourced claims** — "Studies show" without linking to the study

## SEO Best Practices in Writing

### Titles and Headlines

- **Length:** 50–60 characters for page titles, 60 characters max for meta descriptions
- **Front-load keywords** — Put the primary keyword in the first 10 words
- **Brand at the end** — "Strategic Consulting | Acme Co." not "Acme Co. | Strategic Consulting"
- **Unique per page** — No two pages should have identical titles

### Meta Descriptions

- **Length:** 120–155 characters (one short sentence to one medium sentence)
- **Action-oriented** — Describe what the page offers or how it helps
- **Include secondary keyword** if it fits naturally
- **No keyword repetition** — Avoid the same keyword twice

**Good:** "Expert strategic consulting for mid-market manufacturers. Process-driven approach, 5-year track record."

**Bad:** "Strategic consulting services for strategic businesses needing strategic help." (Keyword stuffing)

### Headings (H1, H2, H3)

- **One H1 per page** — The main topic
- **H2 for sections** — Subheadings within the main topic
- **Natural language** — "How to Reduce Churn by 22%" not "Churn Reduction Methodology"
- **Include keywords where relevant** — But don't force them

### Body Copy

- **First 100 words count** — Include primary keyword in first paragraph, naturally
- **Introductory sentence** — Answer "What is this page about?" in one sentence
- **Subheadings every 200–300 words** — Breaks up text, helps scanning
- **Short paragraphs** — 3–4 sentences max; 2–3 on mobile-first pages
- **Lists over paragraphs** — Use bullet points when listing features, steps, or benefits

### Internal Links

- **Descriptive anchor text** — "Learn about our process optimization services" not "Click here"
- **Link to pages that rank or deserve traffic** — Avoid thin or outdated pages
- **3–5 internal links per page** — More doesn't help, fewer misses opportunities
- **Vary anchor text** — Don't link with the same exact phrase every time

### Images

- **Alt text 5–15 words** — Describe what you see, not "image" or "picture"
- **Include keyword only if accurate** — "Consulting team in strategy meeting" not "consulting services strategy consulting team"
- **Decorative images get empty alt** — `alt=""` for spacers, icons, dividers
- **Unique alt text per image** — Don't reuse the same alt on multiple images

## Examples

### Good Blog Post Opening

"Client churn cost us $200K last year. Our retention rate was 71%. After reworking our onboarding process, it climbed to 93% in 6 months. Here's exactly what changed and how to apply it to your team."

**Why:** Specific number, proof, clear benefit, promise of actionable insight.

### Bad Blog Post Opening

"In today's competitive business landscape, customer retention is more important than ever. Companies are constantly looking for innovative ways to improve their churn metrics."

**Why:** Generic, no proof, no specificity, no clear benefit.

### Good Service Page Title

"Strategic Business Consulting for Mid-Market Manufacturers | Acme"

**Why:** Primary keyword up front, audience specified, brand at end, 60 characters.

### Bad Service Page Title

"Acme Consulting Services for Strategic Business and Manufacturing Excellence"

**Why:** Brand first (wrong), keyword diluted, too long (73 characters), no clear audience.

### Good Meta Description

"Reduce operational costs by up to 18% with our process optimization framework. Case studies, methodology, and next steps inside."

**Why:** Specific benefit (18%), actionable promise, scannable list, 135 characters.

### Bad Meta Description

"We provide consulting services to help businesses improve their operations and achieve success through strategic planning."

**Why:** Generic, no proof, keyword stuffing, doesn't say what the page offers.

## Brand Voice Customization

Edit `site-profile.md` to customize tone for your brand:

- **Add adjectives** that match your voice (e.g., "playful, data-driven, direct")
- **List words to avoid** (e.g., "hype language", "corporate jargon", "clichés")
- **Specify audience** (e.g., "technical founders" vs "finance directors") so language matches

Skills will reference your voice rules when writing or editing content.

## When Rules Conflict

- **Readability always wins** — If following these rules makes text harder to read, break the rule
- **Legal/brand requirements override** — If your company requires a specific phrase or tone, that comes first
- **Context matters** — A user testimonial can have different voice than a service page
- **Mobile first** — On shorter screens, shorter sentences and subheadings are even more important

## Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Title is 50–60 characters and includes primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description is 120–155 characters and inviting
- [ ] One H1, multiple H2s that describe sections
- [ ] First paragraph mentions primary keyword naturally
- [ ] No keyword stuffing or repetition
- [ ] Internal links have descriptive anchor text
- [ ] Images have unique, 5–15 word alt text
- [ ] No filler phrases or clichés
- [ ] Specific numbers and dates, not generic claims
- [ ] Active voice used throughout
- [ ] No unsourced claims
